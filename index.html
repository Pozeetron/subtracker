Privacy Policy

SubTracker (Android)
Last updated: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SubTracker (“the App”), published by Evol (“we”, “us”), handles information when you use the App on Android.

1. Summary

SubTracker stores the subscription details you enter on your device. Reminders use optional local notifications and, if you allow it, your device calendar. The App does not require an account and does not upload your subscription list to our servers.

2. Information we process

  • Subscription data you provide: Name, price, currency, billing cycle, next charge date, and whether an item is a free trial. Stored locally on your device.
  • Notifications (optional): If you grant notification permission, the App may schedule a local reminder about two days before a charge date.
  • Calendar (optional): If you grant calendar permission, the App may create a calendar event for a reminder. Calendar content is managed by your device / Google Calendar and is not sent to Evol servers by the App.

3. How we use information

Local data is used only to show your subscription list, estimated monthly totals, upcoming renewals, and optional reminders inside the App.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Subscription entries are not shared with third-party advertisers by the App. Optional calendar events remain under your calendar provider’s policies.

5. Data retention & deletion

Your list remains on the device until you delete individual items, clear App storage, or uninstall SubTracker. Uninstalling removes locally stored subscription data. You may also remove any calendar events the App created using your calendar app.

6. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Security

Protect access to your device with a screen lock. Because data is stored locally, anyone with unlocked access to your phone could view entries you saved in the App.

8. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
